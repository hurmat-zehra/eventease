HOD Login

How do I join an event?

Check the Events page and follow the registration link.

Who can create events?

Only HODs with valid login credentials.

Can I upload event photos?

Only HODs can upload official images via the dashboard.

Is there a fee to attend events?

Most events are free for students, but some may require a registration fee.

Can I edit my registration details?

Yes, you can edit your registration from your profile before the event deadline.

How do I contact the event organizer?

Contact details are available on each event’s page.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes, as long as the event timings don’t clash.

Where can I see past event highlights?

Visit the Gallery section to see images and highlights from past events.

What is the Chatroom used for?

It’s used for student discussions and event-related interactions.

How do I vote in event polls?

Go to the Polls page, log in with your credentials, and submit your vote.
